# Inbox

The Inbox is the capture area for work that has not yet been placed in the task
hierarchy. It combines connected mailboxes, a scratchpad, and the existing task
tree.

## Mail

- Connect one or more Gmail or Outlook accounts through OAuth.
- Choose one connected account at a time to review its unread messages.
- Review up to 25 of the newest unread messages per mail provider.
- Add a message to the scratchpad as a task. Its subject becomes the task name,
  and its sender, received time, and preview are saved in the description.
- Ignore a message without creating a task. Adding or ignoring marks the source
  message as read.
- Refresh, reconnect, or disconnect mailbox accounts from the page.

## Scratchpad

- Enter a task name and press Enter or choose **Add** to capture it.
- Rename or delete captured tasks before organizing them.
- Move a captured task below an existing task with **Move…** or by dragging it
  onto the task tree.

## Task tree

- Create, rename, reorder, nest, expand, and collapse tasks.
- Hide completed tasks or expand and collapse the entire hierarchy.
- Dropping a scratchpad task onto a task makes it a child and removes it from
  the scratchpad.
